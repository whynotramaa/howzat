## Intent

A 75 second cinematic journey of one big night match. Squiggly, boiling multicolour line drawings carry the playfulness; still serif type (Behind The Nineties, Apple Garamond) carries the professionalism. 3D camera moves through a line-drawn stadium.

## Notes

- No captions, no voiceover. Low stadium murmur, SFX, gentle music bed.
- Story: city flight into the stadium, fans watching on phones, last over, back-foot punch for six with a bullet-time orbit, umpire signals six, scorer taps 6, the ball event is queued on the phone, written to the ledger, fanned out to every phone. Storm takes down the broadcast, the link still updates; scorer loses signal, taps queue locally and flush in order. Win, points table, bracket, trophy. Football: strike, net bulge, jump-spin-land celebration. Lines braid into the Howzat mark.
- Real athletes are evoked, never drawn or named: generic kits, no names or numbers.
