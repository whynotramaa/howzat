# Howzat

Howzat lets local cricket and football organizers run tournaments, lets assigned scorers record play, and lets spectators follow a public link without signing in. The scoring console must work on a phone at a ground with unreliable connectivity. PostgreSQL owns durable events; browser queues are recoverable pending work, and Redis is disposable derived data.

The user requests a full redesign and reliability repair, with full autonomy. Keep all existing supported workflows. References pin graphite sports dashboards, restrained chart colors, legible Geist Sans typography, minimal composition, crisp pitch line art, and a bold lime-and-ink sporting identity. The new logo must be generated with ImageGen. Dark and light modes remain available. Assisted scoring uses larger controls and contextual explanations. No invented statistics or simulated predictions may appear as live match facts.
